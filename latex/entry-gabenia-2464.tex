GABENIA, a professor name given to the finest films in the Half Indies, as she from Iow to so per order inferior to them are called barins. The Indian workmans endeavor to pafs them off one with the o-ther, far which reston, the more experienced European merchant take care to open the bales, and to examine all the flains one after another. The Dutch dhlfiguille two forms of cheeks, namely, the more ca-bets, and the common causes. The former is full at Amifterdam for about 215 fechellingen Elemith, and the other for about 184.
